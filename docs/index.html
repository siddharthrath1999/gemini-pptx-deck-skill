How it works

Six steps between your sentence and your slides.

You see one message. Behind it, Gemini works like a small studio: researcher, writer, designer and editor.

01 · Research

Finds the facts

Reads your files first, then searches the web. Every number keeps its source and date.

02 · Story

Writes the storyline

One headline per slide that states the point, so the headlines alone tell the story.

03 · Design

Picks a look

Colours, fonts and slide style chosen for this audience. No two decks need look the same.

04 · Build

Builds real slides

34 layouts, native charts and tables, vector icons and speaker notes, written into a .pptx.

05 · Check

Checks its own work

Tests every slide for cut-off text, overlaps, weak contrast and AI-sounding phrases, then looks at pictures of the slides.

06 · Deliver

Hands you the file

The .pptx plus a short note: what it assumed, any gaps to fill, and its sources.

Questions

Good to know.

What do I need to use it in the Gemini app?

A personal Google account, age 18 or over, and a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan, because the skill runs in Task mode. Upload it once from the Gemini website or Mac app. Task mode isn't offered in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or Nigeria.

Why does it need Task mode?

Task mode gives Gemini a small computer where it can run the skill's tools. In normal Chat mode it can't, so you'd get text, HTML or Google Slides instead of a .pptx.

Can I edit everything?

Yes. Every text box, shape, chart and table is a normal PowerPoint object. Charts keep their data, so you can change numbers in PowerPoint.

Are the numbers right?

Gemini researches and cites its sources, but it can still pick a weak source or misread one. Check any figure you'll present or decide on. The handoff note lists every source.

Can it use my brand colours and fonts?

Colours, yes: give hex codes in your prompt. Fonts default to ones every device has, so the deck looks the same everywhere. Ask for a brand font only if everyone presenting has it installed.

Does it add photos?

No. Visuals come from charts, tables, shapes and icons. Add your own photos in PowerPoint afterwards.

The upload says "did not pass the security scan".

Upload the official zip, unchanged. If you edited files inside it, undo the changes and try again.

"Skill name already exists" when I upload a new version.

Open the skill in Customise, then Skills, click More, then Replace skill, and upload the new zip.

Is this made by Google?

No. PPTX Native is an independent open-source project. Gemini and Antigravity are Google products; their logos appear here only to show where the skill works.
